How to Order

From idea to finished corporate wear.

Start with what you already have - a logo, corporate colours, a design reference, quantity or simply an initial idea.

  1. 01

    Share your requirements

    Tell us your organisation type, quantity, men/women requirements, required date and any design reference you have.

  2. 02

    Choose design & fabric

    Align the colours, panels, collar, sleeves, fabric and logo treatment.

  3. 03

    Confirm measurements / sizes

    Provide a size list or arrange body measurements for the team when required.

  4. 04

    Review & confirm

    Confirm the design details, quantity, sizing, pricing and timeline before production.

  5. 05

    Tailoring / production

    Your order is produced according to the agreed specifications.

  6. 06

    Completion & delivery

    Orders are completed for collection or delivery according to the agreed arrangement.
